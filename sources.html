SOURCE REGISTRY

Sources

A single registry of the public sources used throughout HISTORY. Historical facts are presented from available source data; missing data is not invented.

Wikidata

Structured entities, identifiers, relationships, dates, coordinates, populations and other machine-readable historical data.

Open Wikidata ↗Used by: Map, Search, historical explorer, country data

Wikipedia

Encyclopedic articles and human-readable descriptions used as contextual and fallback information.

Open Wikipedia ↗Used by: Search, country fallback and contextual descriptions

Wikimedia Commons

Images and Wikimedia geographic data, including map files used when historical geographic shapes are available.

Open Wikimedia Commons ↗Used by: Historical map layers and media

OpenStreetMap

Interactive basemap displayed behind the project’s geographic layers.

Open OpenStreetMap ↗Used by: Main interactive map

Internet Archive

Digitized historical collections and archived materials that can be accessed from the project’s archive resources.

Open Internet Archive ↗Used by: Archive and historical material discovery
How source attribution works: individual records can link back to their original public source. The registry above is the project-wide source list; it does not claim that every source contributes data to every record.
